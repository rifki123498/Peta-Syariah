# Copy guide — Sharia Compass voice

Applies to every user-facing string. Checked against public website copy of BSI, BCA Syariah, Bank Muamalat, BTPN Syariah, Prudential Syariah, Pegadaian, BAZNAS, Dompet Dhuafa and Jago Syariah (Nov 2026).

## Contract
- No invented facts, testimonials, user counts, ratings or urgency. Missing data → `[PERLU DATA: …]`.
- Editing never changes numbers, prices, legal or religious references, or what the product does.

## Voice
- Address "kamu" / "-mu", never "Anda". Warm, like a friend who understands money; not a preacher, not a salesperson.
- Landing, ads, captions: casual is fine ("nggak", "kepake", "Yuk,"). Quiz, report, paywall, legal/religious notes: clear standard Indonesian (EYD V).
- Plain words first, term second: "bagi hasil sesuai porsi yang disepakati (nisbah)".
- One idea per sentence; under ~20 words in quiz and microcopy.
- Concrete over abstract. Encourage, never shame.
- Use "Asuransi", not "Perlindungan"/"Proteksi", for the insurance pillar.

## Guardrails
- No halal/haram verdicts on any product, bank, company or person.
- No attack framing against conventional banks; no "bebas riba" as a jab.
- No investment advice or guaranteed returns; mudharabah returns are never fixed.
- Fatwa/POJK numbers copied from `facts-and-sources.md` only.
- Never "diawasi OJK dan DSN-MUI"; say "merujuk fatwa DSN-MUI dan aturan OJK".
- Disclaimer "Panduan edukasi, bukan fatwa" stays on result, report and paywall.

## What sharia institutions do (and we follow)
- Consumer campaigns use "kamu"; service/legal use "Anda".
- Religious meaning is carried by life goals (haji, umrah, rumah, pendidikan, pensiun) and aspirational words (berkah, hijrah, kebaikan), never pressure.
- Invitations, not orders: "Yuk", "Mulai", "Wujudkan". Greeting "Assalamu'alaikum" on personal surfaces (report cover).
- One owned hashtag: #ShariaCompass.
- Akad names usually sit in labels; our edge is explaining them plainly.

## Surfaces
| Surface | Structure |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | Curiosity or pride, e.g. "Coba tebak, berapa persen keuanganmu sudah syariah?" |
| Landing | Problem in the reader's words → what changes → real proof → one CTA |
| Share card | %, level, optional guess, focus pillar, "Berapa persen punyamu?". Never money amounts (privacy, and showing ibadah amounts reads as riya') |
| Paywall | Restate their result, list what unlocks, exact access terms, one CTA, no fake scarcity |
| Microcopy | Verb-first buttons that say the outcome; errors say what happened and what to do |

## Patterns to remove
"Di era digital…", "Tidak dapat dipungkiri", "Penting untuk dipahami bahwa…", "di mana/yang mana" as connectors, nominalisations ("melakukan perbandingan"), adjective triads, more than one "bukan sekadar X, tapi Y" per surface, abstract motivational endings.
